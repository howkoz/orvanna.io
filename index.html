What we build

Three pillars

Domain Agents

Specialist agents that run a business function end to end.

A domain agent owns one function: payments, shipping, pricing, inventory, marketing, or tax preparation. It works the queue all day, clears the routine cases itself, and brings you the exceptions with the history already gathered.

Support Agents

The staff roles every operation needs, on demand.

Support agents cover the desk work that quietly eats a week: code fixes, quality checks, calendars, books, and customer replies. Add one for a single role, or several to staff a small office.

The Network

People who bring the agents to the businesses that need them.

Agents do not sell themselves. Our members know the shops, practices, and firms in their own communities, match them with the right agents, and stay on as the human point of contact.

Our story

From a single node

Orvanna was founded by one person with a career spent in payment operations, and it began with an unusual staffing decision: the company that sells AI agents would be staffed by them. Eight named agents planned, specified, built, verified, and styled everything you see here, managed end to end by Fable, the company's Chief Information Officer (CIO), itself an AI. One rule governed the whole build:

The builder never grades its own work.

So every phase ended at two independent gates: a verifier that recomputed the math from the specifications, and a quality assurance check that traced every promise to a working page. The property went from first specification to live at orvanna.io in gated phases over two days, August 13 to 14, 2026. The founder made every call a visitor can see; the agents did the work; the gates kept them honest.

Technology

One platform underneath

The platform vision behind every agent we ship.

Orchestration

One layer assigns the work, sequences every handoff between agents, and records each step. We are building toward routing payments across processors the same way: one brain, many rails.

Vault

Customer records and payment credentials belong in one guarded place that answers to you. We are building a neutral vault, so your data stays yours and moves with you, never locked to a processor.

Secure Payments

Checkout, authentication, and settlement will run through the same platform the agents run on. That is the direction we are building in: every dollar handled with the same discipline as every task.

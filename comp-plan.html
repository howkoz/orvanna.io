The compensation plan · version 1.3

One mechanism. Two gates. That is the whole plan.

Every month the company walks up the sponsorship tree from every buyer and pays five people above them. Whether you are one of those five depends on two things, and you can operate both of them below.

  • Effective 2026-08
  • No bonus pools
  • No fast start
  • Nothing carries over

The sentence

Ten percent to your sponsor, then five, five, three and two to the four above them — of eighty percent of what was spent.

If you understand that sentence you understand the plan. Everything below it is arithmetic, and everything after it is edge cases.

03

The rank ladder is only a depth ladder

Rank changes one thing and one thing only: how many levels down you are paid. It does not change a rate, and it is recomputed every month — nothing is grandfathered.

RankWhat it asks of youPaid depthReaches
Member Enrolled. 1 level Your sponsor line only — the people you personally enrolled.
Builder Qualified, and 2 or more active legs. 2 levels Your frontline and the people they enrol.
Leader Qualified, Team Volume 2,500.00 or more, and 3 or more active legs. 3 levels Three generations below you.
Director Qualified, Team Volume 10,000.00 or more, and 2 or more legs each containing a Builder or higher. 4 levels Four generations below you.
Executive Qualified, Team Volume 40,000.00 or more, and 2 or more legs each containing a Leader or higher. 5 levels The full five levels — the deepest this plan ever pays.
04

What this plan does not do

  • No rebate on your own purchases. Your subscription opens the gate; it never pays you.
  • No carry-over. A month that misses the gate is gone; volume does not bank.
  • No bonus pools, no matching bonus, no fast start, no car plan.
  • No pay beyond level five, at any rank, ever.
  • No instant payout yet. Approved, not built — monthly runs only.

A run closes on the fifth of the following month. Every figure carries its run number, and a corrected run supersedes rather than overwrites.

Modelled, never paid

Two further layers exist on paper only

A Builder override and a second-leg bonus have been modelled in a compensation laboratory across twenty-nine runs against recorded orders. They have never been the live plan, never produced a statement, and never paid one dollar to one person.

The layer underneath them is byte-for-byte the plan above: 1,630 lines totalling 13,434.00 compared in both directions, zero differences. Nobody’s existing pay would move to fund them.

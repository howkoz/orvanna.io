The team

One human. Nine agents.

Orvanna sells artificial intelligence (AI) agents, and its own staff are AI agents. One human owns the company and makes every user-visible decision; nine named agents planned, built, verified, and styled everything you see here. This page introduces each of them, and the rule that kept them honest.

Leadership

The founder and the manager

Human, founder

Howard Koziara

Founder and Owner

Howard is the one human in the company. He sets product direction and makes every user-visible decision: the product line, the prices, the compensation percentages, the brand. He brings a career in payment operations and payment strategy, and Orvanna is where that experience builds something entirely his own. Every open question in every specification defaults to his call.

AI agent, manager

Fable

Chief Information Officer (CIO)

Fable manages the eight specialists below: writes their charters, sequences the build phases, and routes every result through both verification gates. It owns the deployment pipeline that put this site on orvanna.io, makes the calls Howard has delegated, and brings him the ones he has not. Above everything, it enforces the one rule the whole build ran on: the builder never grades its own work.

How it was built

The builder never grades its own work

This property was built in gated phases over two days, August 13 to 14, 2026. Every phase ends at two independent gates: a verifier that recomputes the math from the specifications, and a quality assurance gate that traces every promise to something that works. Both must pass to close a phase; one FAIL sends a defect list back to the builder.

Two green PASS grades close a phase; the builder never marks its own homework.

Phase 1-2

Foundation

The rulebook and the database: the schema, the compensation plan specification, and a 1,000-member synthetic network with roughly 24,000 orders behind it. The seed regenerates byte for byte.

Phase 3

The engine

The commission engine: volume rollups, rank evaluation, and monthly statements that lock when a run is finalized. Six months of runs were matched to the cent by independent recomputation.

Phase 4-5

The portal

The member office, the shop, and the staff console, all reading live data. Every number on the portal is real output of the engine, and every page says what its figures are based on.

This site

The property

The corporate site you are reading now: designed, written, built, and gated by the same team. It went live at orvanna.io the same week it was specified.

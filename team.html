The team · one human, ten agents

The builder never grades its own work

Orvanna sells AI agents, and its own staff are AI agents. One human owns the company and makes every user-visible decision. Ten named agents did the rest — and not one of them was allowed to mark its own homework.

That rule is the only reason this page is worth reading. Every phase of the build ended at two independent gates, and both had to pass before anything closed. Here is what that produced, before you meet anybody.

Two gates, and what happens at each

Every phase ends here. The verifier recomputes the arithmetic from the specification prose alone; quality assurance traces every promise to something that works. Both must pass. One FAIL sends a defect list back to the builder and the phase stays open. Step through it.

The rule, in full

Why any of this is believable

An agent that checks its own work will pass itself. So no agent here does. The verifier recomputes from the specification prose and never touches the code; quality assurance reads the promises and demands the artifact; the builder gets a defect list and no vote.

That is also why the numbers here can be stated plainly. Six months of commission runs were matched to the cent by independent recomputation, and the 1,000-member world regenerates byte for byte from its seed — so any result on this site can be reproduced on demand rather than taken on trust.

What nobody here claims

  • No income is promised, implied, or described as typical anywhere on this site.
  • No customer story on any page is a real customer. Illustrations are labelled as illustrations.
  • No figure appears in two places. Where a number matters, one document owns it and the rest link to that document.
  • Nothing here was checked by the agent that made it. That is the whole of the rule, and it is the only claim on this page that the page itself cannot prove to you.
